From My Heart
My dearest Sweety,

Before I say anything else — I want to thank God. With everything in me. Because you are not something I deserved or even knew to ask for. You are something He chose to give me, quietly and graciously, in His own perfect timing. And every day I get to be near you, I see His goodness in the most beautiful, human form.

On the 18th of June 2003, God did something wonderful. He brought you into this world — and the world has been more beautiful ever since. Not just for me. For everyone who knows you. But especially for me.

I think about the fact that I came into this world just a few months after you — February 2004 — and I like to believe God was already writing our story before either of us could read. That He had already decided. That somewhere in His plan, the two of us were always meant to find each other.

So on your birthday, I don't just want to celebrate you — I want to praise the God who made you. Because everything you are is His work. And it is the most beautiful work I have ever seen.

"I have loved you with an everlasting love; I have drawn you with unfailing kindness."
Jeremiah 31:3
Everything You Do
Every moment · Every breath · Every you

I love everything about you. And I mean that — everything. There are so many things I notice, so many little things I carry quietly in my heart, that I honestly cannot put them all into words. Some feelings are just too full for language. But I want to try — even just a little — because you deserve to know.

🌅
When you wake up
That sleepy face, those slow blinking eyes — you are most beautiful when the world hasn't touched you yet.
🌬️
When you breathe
Even this. Just the fact that you are alive, somewhere in this world — I am grateful for it every single day.
🚶‍♀️
When you walk
The world keeps going around you but my eyes always find you first. You don't even know you do it — that's exactly why it's so beautiful.
🪑
When you sit
You settle into a moment like you belong exactly there. A quiet grace you carry without even knowing it.
😂
When you laugh
Nothing in this world sounds better. I would do anything — anything — to be the reason for that laugh.
🤫
When you go quiet
I never interrupt. I just wait for you to come back — and every time you do, I fall a little more.
🍽️
When you eat
Although you don't eat much — and honestly the way you picky eat like a little kid, so selective, so particular — I find it so endearing. Only you could make being difficult about food look this adorable.
😴
When you sleep
Every worry gone. Every guard down. Just you, purely and quietly you — painted in peace.
🎶
When you hum or sing
When you don't even realise you're doing it — just softly to yourself — I stop everything and just listen.
😤
When you are upset
Your heart is so full it spills over sometimes. That is never a weakness — that is everything. And I will always be here for it.
🤗
When you hug me
The whole world goes quiet. You have arms that feel like home. I never want to leave.
✨
When you get excited
Eyes wide, talking fast — when something lights you up, you become the most radiant thing I have ever seen.
🙏
When you pray
The way your whole face softens and settles into trust — in those moments I see exactly who God made you to be.
💭
In every ordinary moment
There is not a version of you I do not love. Every single one. I choose all of you — always, without question, without end.

And this list will never be complete. Every day you do something new that I add quietly to my heart. There are not enough words, not enough pages, not enough languages in this world to hold everything I love about you. That is the truth.

Praise & Gratitude
Thank You, Lord.

I want to take a moment on your birthday — not just to celebrate you, but to genuinely praise God for you. Because everything you are — your kindness, your laughter, your faith, your heart — none of it happened by accident. God was intentional about you. He knit you together. He knew your name before the 18th of June ever came.

And then, by His grace, He placed you in my life. That is not something I take lightly. That is something I wake up grateful for every single day — including today, especially today.

A Prayer For Her Health

Lord, I lift her up to You today. You said in Your word — "I am the Lord who heals you" (Exodus 15:26) — and I stand on that promise for her. Let every part of her body be strong and whole. Let her wake up every morning with energy, with health, with the fullness of life You promised her.

Your word says — "Beloved, I pray that you may prosper in all things and be in health, just as your soul prospers" (3 John 1:2). That is my prayer for her, Lord. Every single day. That her body prospers the way her soul does — beautifully, fully, completely.

And "He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds" (Psalm 147:3) — so wherever she carries anything silently, anything unseen, Lord — meet her there. Heal her there. Let this birthday mark the beginning of her healthiest, strongest, most joy-filled year yet. In Jesus' name. Amen.

Lord, bless her this year with more than she can ask or imagine. Give her peace, joy, health, and the fullness of Your love in every single day ahead. She deserves nothing less.

"You are altogether beautiful, my darling — there is no flaw in you."
Song of Solomon 4:7
